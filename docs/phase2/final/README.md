## What the report covers

The report brings together the reviewed work of Sultan Almheiri (100065654), Zayed Alfadli (100064657) and Ghaith Alhinaai (100066185). It includes scope, stakeholder roles, the monitor scenario, market comparisons, public KU evidence, all six feasibility areas, software reuse, one-time and recurring costs, quantified benefits, ROI, payback, risk scores, mitigation, contingency actions and the final recommendation.

There are 38 references and 16 numbered equations. The report and presentation use the same financial model. Source dates distinguish the 22 September baseline, later KU research and the 29 September revision. Figures labelled as assumptions or targets are not measured outcomes.

## Interview and AI disclosure

The instructor allowed an AI role-play approach for the classroom exercise, as reported by the team. The two interviews simulate an asset/procurement coordinator and a department requester. They identify proposed requirements and design decisions. No real KU employee participated. They do not establish actual KU procedures, demand, wages or achieved savings.

The report and presentation disclose the AI assistance used for drafting, research, calculations, role-play, formatting and visuals. The students remain responsible for understanding and checking the submission. There is no separate speaking guide.

## Recommendation

Continue the academic prototype subject to the report's technical, data, operational and capacity conditions. In the base monitor scenario, annual net cash benefit is AED 1,485.57. Once development and support time are included, the three-year ROI is -35.23%. The narrow monitor case therefore does not yet justify a custom live KU rollout.

Real KU workflow and financial inputs need validation before live adoption. Prototype results, cloud usage and staff effort remain unmeasured. Deployment, maintenance, retirement, sustainability and the required AI functions remain in the semester scope.
