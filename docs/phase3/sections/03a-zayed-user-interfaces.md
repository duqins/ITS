# 3. External Interface Requirements

## 3.1 User Interfaces



Dawra Campus shall provide a responsive English website usable in laptop and phone browsers. Screens shall share the same navigation, form conventions and status labels while showing only actions allowed by the user's active role and department. Users shall be able to see which department they are working in and switch among their authorised assignments. This section defines what the screens must do; detailed visual mockups belong to the design phase.

### 3.1.1 Interface Standards

- The system shall use the exact state names in section 4.1, such as Pending department approval, Published and Disputed, and show the next responsible role where an action is outstanding. Colour alone shall not communicate status.
- The system shall show the user's active department and role, consistent navigation, a page title and the unread-notification count. On narrow screens, tables shall remain readable through wrapping or controlled horizontal scrolling.
- Before an approval, rejection, withdrawal, cancellation, handover, receipt or end-of-life completion, the system shall show a confirmation containing the affected record and consequential changes. Rejections, requests for changes and cancellations shall require a reason under BR-20.
- The system shall label generated content as AI suggestion or AI draft, show confidence separately from compatibility, and allow acceptance, correction or rejection where the workflow permits. Unavailable confidence or model-version information shall be marked as unavailable.
- The system shall show an actionable explanation beside invalid fields, retain entered information after validation or service errors, and keep the form available for correction. Permission errors shall not reveal restricted records or credentials.
- The system shall check authorisation on the server as well as controlling visible buttons. A hidden button shall not be treated as the security control, and a confirmed assistant action shall follow the same checks as a classic form (BR-01, BR-15).
- While a submission is being processed, the system shall show its progress and prevent repeated activation from creating duplicate records. It shall recheck the current quantity and approved record version before applying a consequential change.
- The system shall use English labels and help text, visible field labels, plain-language instructions and consistent date formatting as DD Mon YYYY. Money shall show its currency, normally AED; quantities and sustainability figures shall show their units. Audit timestamps shall show the applicable time zone.
- The interface shall target WCAG 2.2 AA, as proposed in the template, with keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, sufficient contrast, meaningful image descriptions and errors associated with their fields. Accessibility shall be verified during testing rather than claimed as already achieved.
- If an AI call fails, times out or returns unusable output, then the system shall explain the issue, preserve entered data and provide the relevant classic alternative under AI-13 and NFR-07. It shall never show a generated answer as an approval.
- The system shall show the proposed values identified in Appendix C as proposed, and identify optional or unavailable features without preventing completion of the essential classic workflow.
